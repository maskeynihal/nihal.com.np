# Research 04: Plain, human copy for an engineer's portfolio

Research date 2026-11-05. Sites fetched live. bruno-simon.com, jacobbijani.com, sarahdrasnerdesign.com unreachable; omitted.

## 2. Principles

- First person, present tense, spoken register. Paul Graham test: would you say it to a friend? Read aloud before shipping.
- Nouns and numbers beat adjectives. Every adjective replaceable by a fact or cut.
- What you did, not what you are. Verbs with objects: built, shipped, migrated, deleted.
- Humor without jokes: specific or understated. No puns, exclamation marks, emoji punctuation.
- Vary sentence length. Uniform punchy sentences are an AI tell too.
- Specificity: tool names, places, years. Unfakeable.
- Admit limits. Reads as confidence.
- Why "Passionate Full Stack Developer crafting seamless digital experiences" fails: every word a claim, none checkable, no proper noun, no number, no verb with object. Pasteable onto 10,000 sites.

**Phrases:** "digital experiences", "user experiences" as product, "I'm a ... based in ...", "Let's connect", "Let's build something together/amazing", "turning ideas into reality", "bringing ideas to life", "at the intersection of", "end-to-end", "from concept to deployment", "Welcome to my portfolio", "Here's the thing", "It's worth noting", "In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "Stay tuned", "Feel free to", "Don't hesitate to reach out", "Coming soon".

**Structural tells:** em-dash overuse (max one per screen); rule-of-three stacking; "Not X. Y." / "No X. No Y. Just Z."; rhetorical question headers; colon-reveal titles; every paragraph ending on a zinger; parallel openers across paragraphs; Title Case everywhere (use sentence case); more than one exclamation mark per page.

## 4. Microcopy for a playful site (first option safest)

- **Reduced-motion toggle:** "Reduce motion" (matches OS, remembered, auto-on with media query) / "Animations: on / off" / "Plain mode. Turns off animation and 3D. Everything still works." Never "Boring mode" alone; OK as secondary: "Reduce motion (boring mode)".
- **Loading:** "Loading 42%" / "Loading the heavy bit. About 2 s on a fast connection." / "One second." Never fake progress or "Reticulating splines".
- **404:** "Nothing here. Try the home page." / "404. That page moved or never existed." / "This URL is wrong, or I broke it. Email me if it's the second one." Never "Oops!", astronauts.
- **Empty:** "No posts tagged 'rust' yet." / "Nothing matched. Try a shorter word."
- **Easter egg hints:** no hint (preferred) / footer "Try pressing ? for shortcuts." / "There are 3 things on this page that react to the keyboard." Never "Psst", winking emoji.
- **Form success:** "Sent. I read everything, usually within a day or two (NPT)." / "Got it. If it's urgent, email works faster."
- **Form error:** "Didn't send. Check the email field and try again." / "Something failed on my end. Email me directly."

## 5. Tone for a Kathmandu engineer, global audience

- City once, as fact: "I live in Kathmandu." No exoticizing, no apologizing, no mountains-and-momos metaphors, no pretending to be in SF. A real work-life detail (load-shedding, traffic) fine if real, not brand.
- Timezone as utility: NPT = UTC+5:45, no DST. "Kathmandu, UTC+5:45. I overlap with Europe mornings and US evenings." Live local clock in footer beats a paragraph.
- English register: write the English you speak. Replace "do the needful", "prepone", "revert back", "kindly".
- Name employer + what it does in one clause (Leapfrog). Name clients/industries that can be disclosed.
- Plain counts, not lakh/crore. One spelling (US) site-wide.
